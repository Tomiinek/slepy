# Colour Vision Test

[👉 Click here to open the test](https://tomiinek.github.io/slepy/)

### 💡 What?

A colour vision test that gives you a proper report that tells you:
- **how strong** your deficiency is
- **which colours** you actually **mix up**
- what everyday **scenes look like** through your eyes
- gives you a **share link**

### 🤔 Why?

If you are colour blind you have answered the same three questions a hundred times. 

> *So you can't see traffic lights?*<br>
> *Do you see in black and white? What colour is this then?*

That is what the **share link** is for. The page it opens is not a copy of your report — it is written
for whoever you send it to. It says:

- who it is about
- shows the colours you confuse
- puts real scenes through your vision
- answers the usual questions up front, traffic lights first
- there is also a section on what to expect if you have children together, since that one comes up too


The idea is to replace the conversation rather than to document it.

> *NOTE:* The share link carries the measurements in the URL itself, so nothing is stored anywhere and links never expire. They come out around 220 characters. You can put your first name in so the page says "Tomas has moderate deuteranomaly" instead of "they have"; that is the only personal thing in it.

### 🫀 How?

Four stages, about six minutes.

1. **Hidden figures:** read the number in a field of coloured dots
2. **Colour sensitivity:** say which side the gap in a ring is on, as the colours get fainter.
3. **Brightness match:** make a red patch as bright as a grey one.
4. **Colour ordering:** sort sixteen swatches into a smooth run.

Every stimulus is generated from colour science, which means the dots and digits change every run so it cannot be memorised, and the difficulty is a dial rather than pass/fail — that is what lets the test measure *how much* rather than just *whether*.
